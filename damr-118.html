Technical Standard 118

Operational incident notification thresholds

Published 17 August 2026 · Reference MMA/TS/118 · Applies from 1 March 2027

Article 1 — Notifiable incidents

An incident shall be notified to the Authority where it results in the unavailability of a core trading, custody or settlement service for a continuous period exceeding sixty minutes, or where it affects more than 5% of active clients in any rolling twenty-four hour window.

Article 2 — Initial notification deadline

An initial notification shall be submitted within four hours of the provider becoming aware of a notifiable incident. The initial notification may be incomplete; a full report shall follow within ten business days.

Article 3 — Register

A provider shall maintain a register of all operational incidents, whether notifiable or not, and shall retain each entry for five years from the date of the incident.
