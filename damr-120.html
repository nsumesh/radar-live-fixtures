Technical Standard 120

Outsourcing registers and critical service providers

Published 28 August 2026 · Reference MMA/TS/120 · Applies from 1 June 2027

Article 1 — Register contents

An authorised provider shall maintain a register of all outsourcing arrangements, recording for each the service provided, the provider's jurisdiction, the date of the agreement, and whether the arrangement supports a critical or important function.

Article 2 — Critical service provider notification

Where an outsourcing arrangement supports a critical or important function, the provider shall notify the Authority no later than thirty days before the arrangement takes effect, and shall include an assessment of the provider's substitutability.

Article 3 — Exit plans

A provider shall maintain a documented exit plan for each critical outsourcing arrangement, tested at least once every twenty-four months, demonstrating that the function can be brought back in house or moved to an alternative provider without interruption to client service.
