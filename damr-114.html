Technical Standard 114

Safeguarding of client funds held by digital asset service providers

Published 10 August 2026 · Reference MMA/TS/114 · Applies from 1 January 2027

Article 1 — Minimum deposit at a credit institution

An authorised digital asset service provider shall hold no less than 60% of aggregate client fiat balances in a segregated account at a credit institution authorised in a member jurisdiction. The remaining balance may be held in qualifying money market instruments with a residual maturity not exceeding ninety days.

Article 2 — Reporting frequency

A provider shall report the composition of safeguarded client funds to the Authority on a monthly basis, within ten business days of the end of each reporting period. The report shall identify each credit institution used and the balance held at each.

Article 3 — Reconciliation

A provider shall reconcile its internal client fund ledger against statements obtained from each credit institution at least once per business day, and shall record any discrepancy exceeding 0.5% of aggregate client balances in its incident register.
