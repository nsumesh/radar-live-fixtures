Custody segregation reviews

The Authority will begin a programme of on-site custody segregation reviews in the fourth quarter. Reviews will focus on whether client asset records permit a same-day reconstruction of individual client positions, and on the adequacy of arrangements with sub-custodians outside member jurisdictions.

Marketing communications

Thematic work on marketing communications found that risk warnings were frequently placed below the point at which a reader is invited to open an account. Providers should expect follow-up requests for evidence that risk disclosures are presented before any invitation to transact.

Authorisation pipeline

Fourteen applications for authorisation remain under assessment. The most common cause of delay continues to be incomplete governance documentation, in particular the absence of a documented outsourcing register.
